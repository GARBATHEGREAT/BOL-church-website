Who we are

More than a service.
A family to call home.

Bread of Life DCM

We are a growing family of believers committed to knowing Jesus, living His Word and sharing His love with our community.

Whatever your story or season, you are welcome here. Come as you are and discover a place to worship, grow, serve and belong. Our purpose is to proclaim the uncompromised Gospel of Jesus Christ, see lives transformed, and help believers grow through God’s Word and the Holy Spirit.

What shapes our ministry

Our Core Values

01

Christ-Centred Living

Jesus Christ is the foundation of all we do (Colossians 1:18).

02

Biblical Truth

The Word of God is our final authority (2 Timothy 3:16–17).

03

Deliverance

Setting captives free through the power of Jesus Christ (Luke 4:18).

04

Divine Healing

Ministering God’s healing power for spirit, soul, and body (Matthew 10:1; James 5:14–16).

05

Prayer

Seeking God’s presence and power through fervent prayer (1 Thessalonians 5:17).

06

Holiness

Pursuing a life that reflects Christ (Hebrews 12:14).

07

Compassion

Demonstrating God’s love through practical service (Matthew 9:36).

08

Discipleship

Raising mature followers of Christ (Matthew 28:19–20).

09

Evangelism

Reaching the lost with the Gospel (Mark 16:15).

10

Excellence and Integrity

Serving God faithfully and with accountability (Colossians 3:23).
